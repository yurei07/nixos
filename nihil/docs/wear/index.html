Install

Wear OS 4 or newer, with a crown, a rotating bezel or a touch bezel. There is no store: NIHIL on the computer puts it on the watch.

  1. On the watch

    Settings, About watch, and tap the build number until the developer options appear; in them, ADB debugging and Wireless debugging, on the computer's Wi-Fi, and Pair new device.

  2. On the computer

    With the deck on, Preferences, Devices, Install on a watch, or:

    nihil watch            # pairs, installs the latest release and its face, invites the watch
nihil watch update     # a newer release on a watch already paired
nihil watch forget     # the watch and the computer forget each other

    It finds the watch by itself, and checks the release against its SHA256SUMS.

  3. The face

    Hold the watch's face and choose NIHIL's.
