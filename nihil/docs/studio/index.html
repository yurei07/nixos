How it's drawn

The desktop's own steps (theme/dotmatrix.py), with the phone's numbers. A picture that moves is drawn frame by frame and leaves as a GIF.

One LED per cell

Cut to the screen's exact size, in cells of 9 px; each cell's light sets its LED.

The night stays off

The black point rises, so a dark sky stays dark.

Stars stay

A single bright point still lights its cell.

Make it your wallpaper

On NIHIL, give it the original picture: it draws it for each screen, at that screen's size.

  1. Into your wallpapers

    PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, named after the file; one that moves keeps moving. One named as one of NIHIL's takes its place.

    sudo mkdir -p /etc/nixos/wallpapers
sudo cp ~/Downloads/my-photo.jpg /etc/nixos/wallpapers/

  2. Rebuild and pick it

    With SUPER W, in Preferences, or by name:

    nihil rebuild
nihil-wallpaper set my-photo

  3. Anywhere else

    The PNG from here is the wallpaper as it is. On a phone, NIHIL for Android has its own studio.

NIHIL is a personal project and is not affiliated with Nothing Technology. Pictures you drop, paste or choose stay in your browser; one given by its address comes from its site, or through wsrv.nl, an open-source image proxy, when the site doesn't allow it.
